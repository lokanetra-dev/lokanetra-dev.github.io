What a report is

A report describes data. It gives no legal, political, financial, security or other professional advice. Check other sources before you base a decision about a person, money, law or safety on one of our reports.

How we make a report

We collect public posts on X about trending topics. A collection holds the posts our tools reached at the time of collection. Deleted posts, protected accounts and posts outside our search terms stay out of it, so each report covers a sample of a conversation.

Language models label the emotion and stance of each post. Models misread sarcasm, slang, code-mixing and regional languages, and they carry the biases of their training data. Read each label as an estimate.

Evidence tiers describe evidence of amplification found in the dataset. A tier proves nothing about automation, payment or coordination. We accuse no account or person of wrongdoing.

DeepSeek drafts the findings section from the report's totals. An analyst checks and edits each draft before we publish it.

Views and names

The views in a report belong to its authors. A report names an account, person, group, brand or party to describe the data. Lokanetra neither endorses nor opposes anyone it names. People post for many reasons, and a place in a report says nothing about an account's intent.

Third-party services

Cloudflare and GitHub Pages deliver this site. Zenodo holds our datasets. DeepSeek, Cerebras or Together AI process post text for our labels. Each company runs its own service under its own terms. We endorse none of them, and none of them reviews or endorses our reports. Our privacy policy lists what each one handles.

Corrections

If you find an error, email contact@lokanetra.dev with the address of the report. We fix confirmed errors and say what changed in the report's remarks.
