Lokanetra Information Observatory runs as a non-profit. We publish the reports at open.lokanetra.dev/reports for education and research on public conversation in Indonesia. We charge nothing for them and carry no advertising. You may use them for teaching, study and non-commercial research under our terms of service.

What a report is

A report describes data. It gives no legal, political, financial, security or other professional advice. Check other sources before you base a decision about a person, money, law or safety on one of our reports.

How we make a report

Each report rests on a sample of public posts on X and on automated analysis. Samples miss posts, and automated tools make mistakes. Read every figure and label as an estimate.

No finding proves that an account is automated, paid or coordinated. We accuse no account or person of wrongdoing. Analysts review each report before we publish it.

Views and names

The views in a report belong to its authors. A report names an account, person, group, brand or party to describe the data. Lokanetra neither endorses nor opposes anyone it names. People post for many reasons, and a place in a report says nothing about an account's intent.

Third-party services

Cloudflare and GitHub Pages deliver this site. Zenodo holds our datasets. Third-party language model services help us analyse posts. Each company runs its own service under its own terms. We endorse none of them, and none of them reviews or endorses our reports. Our privacy policy says what data they receive.

Corrections

If you find an error, email contact@lokanetra.dev with the address of the report. We fix confirmed errors and say what changed in the report's remarks.
