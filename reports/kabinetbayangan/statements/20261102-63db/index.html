[Mockup] Food safety in the free school meals programme

Health, raised 20 October 2026

The issue

Raised 20 October 2026 by Kabinet Bayangan health desk

Kabinet Bayangan asks how the government checks food safety in the free school meals programme after reports of pupils falling ill.

  • Who inspects the kitchens that cook the meals?
  • How fast does the government publish the results?
  • What happens to a kitchen that fails a check?

What the government says

22 October 2026, Spokesperson, Badan Gizi Nasional (BGN). Source

The agency says every kitchen in the programme follows a standard operating procedure, and that regional teams will check kitchens in the affected regions this month.

Mockup record to show the layout. Not a real statement.

References

  • Badan Gizi Nasional

What Kabinet Bayangan says

24 October 2026, Shadow minister for health, Kabinet Bayangan. Source

Kabinet Bayangan replies that a procedure on paper does not show that kitchens follow it. It asks the government to publish the result of each kitchen check within seven days.

Mockup record to show the layout. Not a real statement.

Action taken by the government

29 October 2026, Head of agency, Badan Pengawas Obat dan Makanan (BPOM). Source

The agency starts sampling meals from kitchens in two regions and sends the samples to its laboratories.
