Danantara-related buzzer activity on Danantara Trust Indonesia launch

Published 19 October 2026

#BaktiNyataDanantaraDanantara Trust Indonesia

Download PDF

Introduction

On 17/9/2026, we have detected a significant activity on Twitter on a possible amplification of certain keywords related to Danantara Trust Indonesia. Two keywords that came to our attention are "Danantara Trust Indonesia" and #BaktiNyataDanantara. At a glance, these two keywords can be perceived as an amplified trending topic. We suspect this amplification was carried out to build a perception of public trust in relation to the philanthropic arm of Danantara.

Findings

  • The dataset covers 296 tweets from 294 unique accounts between 18/10/2026 08:55 and 18/10/2026 18:23 GMT+7. It records 296 original tweets and 30 engagements: 16 likes, 7 retweets and 7 comments. The two most active accounts, yulilianaaa and lula30001, each post 2 tweets; the other eight listed accounts each post 1.
  • #baktinyatadanantara appears in 294 tweets. Top words include danantara 386, indonesia 382, trust 316, masyarakat 121, pembangunan 111, program 91 and dana 62. The text concentrates on the hashtag and these terms.
  • The busiest hour is 08:00 GMT+7 on 18/10/2026 with 137 tweets. The dataset records 0 burst hours.
  • Emotion labels cover 148 tweets, the top 50% by reactions. Trust appears in 77.0%, Anticipation in 49.3%, Joy in 21.6%, Surprise in 2.0%, Disgust in 0.7%, Anger in 0.7%, Fear in 0.0% and Sadness in 0.0%.
  • Stance coding on 148 tweets finds 98.6% agree that the tweet aims to create an image of public trust in Danantara, 0.0% disagree and 1.4% are not related.
  • The dataset marks 53 accounts as metadata-suspicious (T2), 241 as no signals (T0), and 0 accounts as burst participation only (T1) or coordinated (T3). It records 0 burst hours. These tiers describe evidence found in the dataset, not proof of automation, payment or coordination.

Remarks

From the burst hour and minimum interaction on the map, we concluded from the findings that there is a high chance that the keywords listed above are artificially inflated to enter the keywords in the top 50 list of trending topics.
