Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026.

Lokanetra Information Observatory ("Lokanetra", "we") publishes research reports at open.lokanetra.dev/reports. This policy covers two groups of people: you as a visitor, and the people whose public posts appear in our data. Write to contact@lokanetra.dev with any question or request.

Visitors

You can read every report without an account. We ask for no name or email address. We run no analytics or advertising on the report pages and set no cookies of our own there. The report pages load their fonts, images and scripts from open.lokanetra.dev and from no other server.

The home page at open.lokanetra.dev stores your theme and your cookie choices in your browser's local storage. Your browser keeps them on your device. Clear the site data in your browser to remove them.

If you email us, we keep your message and your address to answer you. We delete them 12 months after our last reply.

Services that handle your visit

Two companies sit between your browser and our pages. Each one receives your IP address, the address of the page you open, your browser's user agent and the time of your request. We never receive their logs. Cloudflare shows us traffic totals, such as requests per day and the countries visitors come from.

People in our data

Our reports study public conversation on X (formerly Twitter) about trending topics in Indonesia. For each topic we collect public posts. A collected post carries its text, its time, the account's handle and display name, and public counts such as likes, reposts, replies and followers. We collect no private messages and no posts from protected accounts.

A report page shows account handles beside their counts, for example the accounts that posted most about a topic. Some charts show our model labels, such as emotion, for each account. When we publish a report's dataset on Zenodo, the dataset holds the collected posts with their text and handles.

We use this data for non-profit research, statistics and education about public debate. We rely on our legitimate interest and the public interest in that research. We sell no data, build no advertising profiles and never contact the accounts we study.

To label the emotion and stance of each post, we send post text to one language model service per run. To draft a report's findings, we send DeepSeek the report's totals and the handles of the most active accounts.

Your rights

Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) lets you ask what data we hold about you, correct it, delete it or object to our use of it. If you live under another law, such as the GDPR in the European Union, you can use the rights it gives you too.

To make a request, email contact@lokanetra.dev with your handle and the address of the report. Show us that the account belongs to you, for example with a post or a direct message from it. We reply within 72 hours.

If we delete your data, we remove your handle from the report pages and from our copies of the data at the next site update. For a dataset on Zenodo, we publish a new version without your data and ask Zenodo to hide the old files.

You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.
